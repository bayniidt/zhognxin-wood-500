# Export & Logistics Behaviors

- Header uses the existing responsive navigation: full navigation above 600px, menu toggle below 600px.
- The source is a linear information page with no tabs, accordions, carousels, or click-driven content panels.
- External interactions are the Google Maps, WhatsApp, and email links.
- Responsive layout collapses two-column content to one column at 768px; process cards collapse from three columns to two and then one at 500px.
- Browser automation was unavailable in this environment, so screenshots and computed-style extraction could not be captured.
